Online interior design

A step by step insight below.

01 Step one

Gathering the brief

It starts with a questionnaire and an online chat. This is where we fact find and pull your brief together.

02 Step two

Photos and measurements

At this point we need some photographs and measurements of the space, don't worry about the measuring as we have a handy guide to show you how and what we need.

03 Step three

Furniture layout

Once we have gathered all the info we begin by planning the space using a 2D floorplan and making sure existing or new furniture fits. We won’t proceed from here until you are fully happy with how the space flows.

04 Step four

We work on your scheme

When the floorplan is approved its time for you to sit back and relax whilst we work on your interior scheme, expect paint & flooring suggestions, moodboards, an interactive shopping list, handy guides and more.

05 Step five

Shopping & implementing

Once you have received your interior presentation you will be equipped to implement the design and shop all your scheme products via the interactive shopping list included. We will still be on hand at this point for any support you need.
