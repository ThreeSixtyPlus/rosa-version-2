Who is behind Rosa Rose

Hi, nice to meet you

Thanks for stopping by! We are Emma and Sarah, two friends who met while studying for a BA (Hons) degree in Interior Design at university 19 years ago.

We are passionate about how spaces make you feel. It's the reason we started Rosa Rose Design.

We both have valuable experience in the design industry and want to share our skills and knowledge in an affordable way. Online design lets us do that, making great design accessible for everyone.
